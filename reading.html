Legend

  • 👑 - one of the best, my personal favorites
  • ‼️ - really good book that is a must-read
  • ❗ - good book I highly recommend
  • (blank) - good book, enjoyable but probably unremarkable
  • 🥺 - guilty pleasure; not necessarily a good book, but there’s something about it
  • 👎 - not a good book, I would not recommend
  • 🤮 - a really bad book I did not finish
